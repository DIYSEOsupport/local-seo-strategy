📍 Local SEO Strategy Checklist

Welcome to your local SEO strategy dashboard! Use the core pillars below to optimize your business visibility online.

🚀 Essential Action Items

  • Google Business Profile: Claim, verify, and complete 100% of your business details.
  • On-Page SEO: Optimize your website headers, titles, and content for local city keywords.
  • Local Citations: Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across Yelp, YellowPages, and social media.
  • Reviews & Reputation: Set up a system to gather and respond to customer reviews consistently.
